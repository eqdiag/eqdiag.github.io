Fourier And His Friends [~root]

This post is intended to be the first in a series of notes covering a concise summary and overview of linear systems and Fourier theory. The goal here is at first to just have a place to return to for myself when I want to review some of these topics. The notes will be split into 4 parts:

  • basics of systems (also called operators)
  • Fourier series and transform for continuous signals
  • Fourier series and transform for discrete signals
  • The Laplace transform
